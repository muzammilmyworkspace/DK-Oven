The DK's Promise

Danish Khan ka oven sirf pizza nahi banata. It bakes passion into every slice: fresh dough, melty cheese, juicy chicken, and a crust that crackles when you bite in.

From Dough to Your Door

01

Fresh Dough

Every morning our dough is kneaded fresh, rested, then hand-stretched into a soft, airy base. No frozen shortcuts, ever.

02

Signature Sauce

A rich, tangy tomato sauce, made in-house and swirled on from the centre out so every bite gets its share.

03

Mozzarella Rain

A generous blanket of shredded mozzarella. Kanjoosi nahi, cheese poora dalta hai.

04

Loaded Toppings

Tikka chicken, juicy tomatoes, crunchy capsicum, onions, olives and jalapeños, piled edge to edge.

05

Into the Fire

Into the blazing oven until the crust turns golden and the cheese bubbles and browns. Khushboo poore mohalle mein.

06

Slice & Pack

Sliced clean and sealed hot in a DK's box, so it arrives exactly the way it left the oven.
